Batch 02 · Vanilla track · Hero V10

Four moving parts. No framework, no bundler, one vendored library.

01 · The press

Each headline plate is a real DOM element animated by motion.animate() with a spring transition and motion.stagger() as the delay. Overlap is the whole effect: the second plate is still travelling when the third starts, so the type reads as a mechanical press rather than a fade.

02 · The ink

The halftone is not an image. A cell grid is measured to the viewport, each cell gets a density from animated value noise plus a lobe around the pointer, and the squares are batched into three alpha groups before a single fill() per group. The canvas multiplies onto the paper, so paper texture survives.

03 · The binding

motion.scroll() reports page progress; the marquee integrator turns its derivative into band velocity, the plates take a skew, and the stamp badge spins harder the faster you move. Scroll is the only source of truth — the bands never animate on their own timer.

04 · The contract

Without JavaScript the type is simply set. With reduced motion the press holds: one halftone frame, static bands, no springs. Tiers scale the cell size and the spring stiffness, and a one-shot degrade coarsens the grid if the frame rate collapses.
