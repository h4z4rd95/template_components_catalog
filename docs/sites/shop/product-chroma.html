Why the colour moves

The finish is a thin-film interference stack, not a dye. Its apparent hue depends on the angle you hold it at — which is exactly why this page is a stage instead of a photograph. Nothing here is a gradient baked into an image; the swatch is computed from the same numbers as the object.

Made in one workshop
